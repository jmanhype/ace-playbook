# MAGNITUDE Episode 1 — Audio Concept

Project: **MAGNITUDE Episode 1 Last Known Footage**  
Format: **60s recovered 16mm classified military reel**  
Observer: **PFC Tanaka**  
Dialogue rule: **No scripted dialogue. No narration. No readable human lines.** Human voices are only distant, degraded, nonverbal, or buried radio texture.

---

## Audio Philosophy

The sound should feel like the camera's last memory: procedural harbor normalcy slowly crushed by scale, pressure, and electromagnetic overload. The audience should believe the audio was recovered from damaged optical/magnetic 16mm evidence, not designed as a modern trailer.

Arc: **ambient harbor → impossible water suction → emergence pressure → energy buildup → silence before impact → tape/film overload → black.**

## Sound Layers

### 1. Archive Bed
- 16mm projector/transport flutter
- optical track hiss
- occasional splice tick
- mild wow/flutter during shockwaves
- final emulsion burn crackle and static collapse

### 2. Harbor Reality Layer
- gulls, ropes, water slap, chains, truck idle, boots on dock planks
- work sounds fade as soldiers stop responding
- keep period-authentic: no modern alarms, no clean digital radio beeps

### 3. Water Recede Layer
- unnatural suction like a harbor inhaling
- mud slurp, ropes tightening, hulls settling
- low-frequency negative-pressure pulse under the sound field

### 4. Creature Layer
- not a stock roar first; begin with sub-bass chest cavity resonance
- water pouring off massive hide
- dock groans responding to footstep shock
- one enormous inhale before atomic breath

### 5. Sirens and Radio Texture
- distant base sirens start late in shot 003
- radio chatter should stay unintelligible and background-only
- use **Fish S2 Pro** for degraded military radio chatter background: clipped, narrow-band, period-flavored, no clear story dialogue

### 6. Foley From Rendered Pixels
- use **Sony Woosh** for foley extracted from rendered motion: water sheets, camera handling, tripod impacts, dock cable tension, blast air displacement
- foley should follow the rendered pixels, not lead them

### 7. Score / Drone
- use **Stable Audio 3.0** for a dark military tension drone building to catastrophe
- no melody, no heroic brass, no trailer percussion
- texture references: sub-bass pressure, bowed metal, low male choir-like air, distant war-room dread

## Mix Notes

- Keep most of the piece quieter than a trailer; the terror is scale and archival realism.
- Let low frequencies carry @Magnitude's mass, but avoid clipping until the atomic breath failure.
- The silence before impact must be obvious and uncomfortable.
- Final card can carry only thin hiss/static tail, then black silence.
