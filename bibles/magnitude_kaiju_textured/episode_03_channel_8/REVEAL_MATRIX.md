# MAGNITUDE Reveal Matrix

Full-body visibility is not universally banned. Visibility is determined by the recording medium.

| Recording medium | Canonical reveal rule |
|---|---|
| Military 16mm | Fragment preferred |
| Naval 16mm | Fragment preferred |
| Remote weather camera | Full silhouette allowed |
| Local television broadcast | Full body allowed through severe signal degradation |
| Clean cinematic camera | Prohibited |
| Close-range civilian footage | Incomplete, unstable, or obstructed |

## Channel 8 rule

Shot 3 may reveal the complete organism because the image passes through a fixed distant weather camera, local-TV broadcast chain, CRT display, and off-air VHS recording. Full visibility must be degraded by skyline occlusion, tower lattice, weather haze, poor exposure, scanlines, interlacing, ghosting, tracking damage, and chroma separation.

The recording medium must humiliate the image. It may reveal the creature, but it may not present the creature.

**Tagline:** The signal bent before anyone understood why.
