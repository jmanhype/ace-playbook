# SGFLIX Character Pack — Spirit Detective / Dark Tournament Inspired

Purpose: a legally safer SGFLIX-original character roster inspired by the *energy* of classic supernatural battle anime: street fights, spirit courts, demon tournaments, rival crews, mentors, and moral corruption arcs. These are not direct copies of existing characters.

## Core Visual Rules

- Format: cinematic anime-real hybrid, high contrast, rain, neon, alley fog, spirit aura glow.
- Camera language: low-angle hero shots, tournament closeups, fast hand-to-hand impacts, aura flares.
- Wardrobe: late-80s/90s streetwear, school jackets, battle uniforms, underworld formalwear.
- Motifs: spirit flame, demon flora, shadow blades, honor sigils, ferry lanterns, arena dust.
- Avoid: exact names, exact costumes, exact hair/costume combinations from copyrighted characters.
